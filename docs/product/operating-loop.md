# The Founderive operating loop

Founderive helps an early-stage founder turn uncertainty into the next evidence-backed action. It runs on the founder's own computer and keeps everything as plain Markdown files the founder owns. A founder can use it alone, in a program, or by talking to an AI assistant that reads and writes the same files.

## The loop

> **Assumption → Test → Evidence → Learning → Decision → Next commitment**

Every part of the app either moves this loop forward or keeps a useful record of it. Activity is not progress: a completed worksheet is participation, and a canvas block is a belief, not a fact.

## Principles

- **Evidence before completeness.** What customers did and paid counts for more than what they said. Paid beats behavior, and behavior beats opinion.
- **One meaningful weekly outcome.** Each week has one outcome. Commitments support it; a long checklist doesn't replace it.
- **Stage-aware guidance.** The suggested next step depends on stage, business model and the evidence already logged, not on a fixed curriculum.
- **The founder decides.** An assistant may draft, summarize and propose links. The founder confirms interpretations ("what this changes"), test results and decisions.
- **Stopping is a valid result.** Continue, revise, pause and stop are all evidence-based decisions. A test that disproves a belief is progress.
- **Programs are optional.** A program is a schedule laid over the same journey, never a requirement.

## Records

| Record | File | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Founder profile | `founder.md` | Venture, stage, business model, target customer, 30-day outcome, weekly hours |
| Weekly check-in | `checkins/YYYY-Www.md` | The week's outcome, commitments, blocker, help needed, and the founder's "not now" choices (weeks start Monday) |
| Canvas | `canvas/<block>.md` | One current belief per block, with every earlier wording kept |
| Conversation | `conversations/YYYY-MM-DD-name.md` | Who, what they said and did, numbers, alternatives, follow-ups, linked tests, and what it changes (confirmed by the founder) |
| Venture test | `tests/YYYY-MM-DD-name.md` | Belief, risk, the test, success and failure criteria set in advance, linked evidence, evidence strength, then a result and a separate decision |
| Decision | `decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-name.md` | Decisions made outside a test: belief before, evidence, alternatives, the decision, why, and whether it's reversible |
| Program week (optional) | `program/week-NN.md` | Assignments, each done or skipped with a reason |

Customer and prospect conversations are evidence when the founder has confirmed them and they record something: notes, what the person does today, a quote or a number. Mentor, investor and partner conversations are advice: they can be linked to a test, but they're labeled as advice and never count as customer evidence. A conversation counts on a test only when both records list each other; a link only one side makes shows as needing repair.

## The next action

Today shows one recommended action with a one-line reason. It's computed by one function, in this order:

1. A cash, legal, safety or delivery risk
2. A missing weekly outcome, or a blocker on it
3. A running venture test
4. An overdue commitment
5. A follow-up that is due
6. Any other open commitment that isn't overdue, earliest due first: do what you said you'd do before taking on a new guide step
7. A commitment made for a guide step or chosen when completing a test (while not overdue)
8. The next guide step, unless a guide commitment is open. It completes from logged evidence rather than a checkbox
9. Program work
10. Fixing a file Founderive can't read

Open commitments from earlier weeks carry into Today, labelled with their week, and rank exactly as this week's do. They stay in the check-in they were written in; marking one done or dropped updates that file.

The founder can skip a recommendation for the week, defer it to a date, or replace it with something else. Each choice needs a reason and is kept in that week's check-in. Events and journal entries never outrank these.

## Capacity

Each open commitment counts as about three hours. When the week's open commitments clearly exceed the founder's stated hours, Today asks whether this week's load fits; the founder decides.

## Progress

Progress is shown as separate kinds, never one score:
- **Participation:** sessions attended, assignments done, check-ins written.
- **Learning:** customer conversations, tests completed, evidence strength, decisions.
- **Commercial:** offers made, paid pilots and sales, revenue, repeat customers.
- **Business health:** cash and runway, overdue receivables, launch gaps, capacity.

File counts, time in the app and finished assignments never count as validation.

## Privacy

- The record lives in a folder on the founder's computer. Nothing is shared automatically.
- An assistant's writes are visible in the app as soon as they land, and the founder confirms anything that counts as interpretation or decision.
- Imported drafts are marked as imported until the founder confirms them.
- Feedback sent from the app contains only what the founder types, the page and the app version.
